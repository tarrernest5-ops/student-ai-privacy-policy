Privacy Policy

Student AI Assistant

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Student AI Assistant handles information when you use the app. The information processed depends on the features you choose to use.

1. Information that may be processed

  • Google Sign-In: If you sign in with Google, the app receives account information such as your Google account email address and display name, along with authentication information used to verify the sign-in.
  • Profile information: Information you enter, such as your name, school, country, education level and selected subjects. The app also stores a profile-image file path on your device.
  • Learning activity: Quiz attempts, scores, question totals, correct-answer totals, completed quiz details and related progress information.
  • AI requests: Questions, selected subjects and other content you submit to the app's AI features.
  • Photos: Images you choose from your gallery or take and submit for AI homework assistance.
  • Voice input: Voice input when you activate the app's speech-to-text feature. The Android app requests microphone access for this feature.
  • Advertising information: Advertising-related information may be processed by Google AdMob when ads are served, subject to Google's services and settings.

2. How information is used

Information is used to provide app features, authenticate sign-ins, personalize the learning experience, save quiz progress, answer questions, process submitted homework photos, support voice input, maintain and secure the service, and display ads where enabled.

3. AI processing and service providers

Student AI Assistant uses an online backend to handle certain requests. Depending on the feature and service availability, submitted questions may be processed by Google Gemini or Cloudflare Workers AI. Photos submitted for homework assistance are sent to the app's backend and forwarded to Google Gemini for processing.

Google Sign-In is used to authenticate users. Google AdMob is integrated for advertising. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. The app developer does not control the independent data practices of those providers.

4. Photos and microphone

You choose whether to submit a photo or activate voice input. A submitted photo is transmitted to the app's backend for AI processing. Microphone access is used for speech-to-text input when you activate that feature. You can manage app permissions through your device settings; disabling a permission may make the related feature unavailable.

5. Storage and retention

Some profile and learning information is stored locally on your device using app storage. Information you submit for online features is transmitted to the app's backend and relevant service providers to process your request. Retention periods may depend on the service provider and its applicable policies. This policy does not promise that information processed by an independent provider is immediately deleted.

6. Sharing

The app does not sell your personal information. Information may be transmitted to the service providers described in this policy when necessary to provide sign-in, AI processing, hosting or advertising features. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or to protect the security and integrity of the service.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the app. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose whether to use Google Sign-In, submit photos, or activate voice input. You can manage microphone permissions through your device settings. For questions or requests about information handled by the app, contact the developer at the email below. Requests concerning information held by third-party providers may also need to be made directly to those providers.

9. Children's privacy

Student AI Assistant is an educational app. The developer does not knowingly intend to collect personal information from children under 13 through features directed specifically at them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information, contact the developer so the concern can be reviewed.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features, service providers or data practices change. The updated policy will be published at this webpage with a revised date.
