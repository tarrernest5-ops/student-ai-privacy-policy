Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of information associated with Student AI Assistant.

1. Request deletion by email

Send an email to tarrernest5@gmail.com with the subject Student AI Assistant Data Deletion Request.

Include the Google account email address used in the app so that we can identify the relevant information. Do not send your Google password, sign-in code, or authentication token.

We aim to review and process valid requests within 30 days. We may contact you if we need reasonable information to verify the request.

2. Information stored on your device

The app can store profile details (such as your name, school, country, education level and subjects), Google account name and email, a profile-photo path, and quiz progress or results on your device.

You can clear profile and learning information from the app's Privacy & Data screen. To remove all app data saved on an Android device, including the locally saved Google account name and email, use Android Settings to clear Student AI Assistant's app storage. Menu names may vary by device.

3. Information sent to online services

When you use online AI features or Google sign-in, information may be sent to the app's backend and relevant service providers to provide those features. A deletion request allows us to review information under our control. Copies or technical records held by third-party providers may be subject to their own retention and deletion policies.

4. What this request does not delete

Deleting information associated with Student AI Assistant does not delete your Google account. To manage your Google account or its access to third-party apps, use your Google Account settings.

The app's current Privacy & Data controls clear profile information and learning records separately. They are not a Google account deletion button. Contact us by email for a deletion request concerning information associated with your use of the app.

5. Contact

For questions about privacy or deletion requests, contact tarrernest5@gmail.com.
