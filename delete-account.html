STUDENT AI ASSISTANT

Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of your Student AI Assistant account and information associated with your use of the app. You do not need to reinstall the app to submit a request.

1. How to submit a deletion request

Send an email using the link below. Use the subject Student AI Assistant Account and Data Deletion Request.

Email a deletion request to tarrernest5@gmail.com

In your message, include the Google account email address you used with Student AI Assistant so that the request can be matched to the correct app use. Do not send your Google password, sign-in code or authentication token. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you are making the request.

We aim to review and process verified requests within 30 days. If a request is complex or requires additional verification, we will contact you. We will delete information under the developer's control and, where applicable, request deletion of information processed by service providers on the developer's behalf.

2. Information covered by a request

A request may cover information associated with your use of Student AI Assistant, including Google account details received by the app, profile information, and learning information held by the developer. Information stored only on your device may need to be cleared separately as described below.

3. Information stored on your device

The app may store profile details (such as your name, school, country, education level and selected subjects), Google account display name and email, a profile-image file path, and quiz progress or results on your device.

Use the app's Privacy & Data controls, where available, to clear profile and learning information. To clear all app data saved on an Android device, you can use Android Settings to clear Student AI Assistant's app storage. Menu names may vary by device. Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored app data, depending on your device settings and backups.

4. Online AI requests and service providers

Questions and photos submitted for AI features are sent to the app's backend and may be forwarded to Google Gemini or Cloudflare Workers AI to process your request. Google services are also used for sign-in and may provide speech recognition. AdMob processes advertising-related information. When a deletion request is received, the developer will review information under the developer's control and request deletion from service providers when applicable. Independent providers may retain some information under their own legal, security or service policies.

5. Information that is not deleted by this request

Deleting your Student AI Assistant account does not delete your Google account. To manage your Google account itself or its access to third-party apps, use your Google Account settings. Information that must be retained for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention or compliance may be kept for the period required for that purpose.

A request to delete your Student AI Assistant account is separate from a request to delete your Google account. Do not include passwords or authentication codes in your deletion email.

6. Contact and privacy policy

For questions about this process, contact tarrernest5@gmail.com.
